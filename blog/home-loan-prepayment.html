Home Loan Prepayment: Fayda ya Nuksan?

Prepay karne se pehle yeh zaroor padhein

Last updated: January 2026 | Reading time: 6 minutes

Home loan ek lambi commitment hoti hai — 15, 20, ya 30 saal ki. Aur is duration mein aap loan amount se zyada interest bharte hain. Isliye har koi sochta hai — "Home loan prepay kar doon?"

Lekin prepayment hamesha faydemand nahi hota. Kayi factors hain jo decide karte hain ki prepay karna chahiye ya nahi. Chaliye detail mein samjhein.

Prepayment Ke Fayde

1. Interest Bachat

Sabse bada fayda — interest bachta hai. Jaise ₹30 lakh loan, 8.5% interest, 20 saal ke liye:

ScenarioTotal InterestBachat
Prepayment nahi₹32,46,000—
₹5 lakh prepay (5th year)₹21,80,000₹10,66,000
₹10 lakh prepay (5th year)₹14,50,000₹17,96,000

Dekha? ₹5 lakh prepay karne se ₹10 lakh se zyada interest bach sakta hai!

2. Loan Jaldi Khatam

20 saal ka loan 12-15 saal mein khatam ho sakta hai. Isse mental peace milti hai.

3. EMI Kam Ho Sakti Hai

Prepayment ke baad aap EMI kam kar sakte ho ya tenure kam kar sakte ho. Tenure kam karna zyada faydemand hai.

4. Credit Score Improve

Loan jaldi chukane se credit score improve hota hai — future loans ke liye faydemand.

Prepayment Ke Nuksan

1. Prepayment Charges

Kuch banks floating rate loans par prepayment charges lete hain (0-2%). Fixed rate loans par 2-3% tak. Lekin RBI ke rules ke hisaab se floating rate home loans par koi charge nahi hona chahiye.

2. Tax Benefits Khatam

Home loan par aapko Section 24(b) mein ₹2 lakh tak interest deduction milta hai. Prepay karne se yeh benefit kam ho jata hai.

Agar aap 30% tax slab mein hain, to ₹2 lakh deduction se ₹60,000 tax bachta hai — matlab effective interest rate kam ho jata hai.

3. Liquidity Kam

Prepay karne se aapke paas emergency fund kam ho jata hai. Yeh risky hai.

4. Better Investment Opportunity Miss

Agar aapko 12-15% return mil raha hai kisi investment mein, aur home loan 8.5% ka hai — to invest karna better hai prepay karne se.

Kab Prepay Karna Chahiye?

Prepay karo agar:

  • Aapke paas idle cash hai (FD mein pada hai)
  • Interest rate 8% se zyada hai
  • Aap risk-averse ho
  • Aap loan-free hona chahte ho
  • Aap retirement ke kareeb ho

Prepay mat karo agar:

  • Aapke paas emergency fund nahi hai
  • Aap equity investments mein 12%+ return expect karte ho
  • Aap 30% tax slab mein ho aur interest deduction claim kar rahe ho
  • Loan par prepayment charges hain
  • Aapke paas better investment opportunity hai

Smart Prepayment Strategy

Strategy 1: Partial Prepayment Har Saal

Har saal bonus milne par ₹1-2 lakh prepay karo. Isse loan tenure dramatically kam ho jata hai.

Strategy 2: Tenure Kam Karo, EMI Nahi

Jab prepay karo, to EMI kam karne ke bajaye tenure kam karo. Isse zyada interest bachta hai.

Strategy 3: 50-50 Rule

Bonus ka 50% prepay karo, 50% invest karo. Best of both worlds.

Strategy 4: Refinance + Prepay

Agar interest rate 9%+ hai, to refinance karke 8% wale bank mein shift ho jao, phir prepay karo.

Conclusion

Prepayment ek personal decision hai. Koi ek rule sabke liye nahi hai. Apni situation, tax slab, aur investment opportunities dekh kar decide karo.

Simple rule: Agar aapki post-tax interest rate (8.5% - tax benefit) aapke investment returns (12%) se kam hai, to invest karna better hai. Warna prepay karo.
