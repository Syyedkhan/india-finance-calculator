Salary Structure: CTC se In-Hand Kaise Calculate Karein?

Salary slip ka pura breakdown — aasan bhasha mein

Last updated: January 2026 | Reading time: 7 minutes

Jab aapko job offer milta hai, to company CTC (Cost to Company) batati hai. Lekin CTC aur aapki in-hand salary mein bada farak hota hai. Is article mein hum salary structure ko pura samjhenge — taaki aap apni salary slip dekh kar confuse na ho.

CTC Kya Hai?

CTC (Cost to Company) matlab company aap par kul kitna kharch karti hai — salary + benefits + PF + gratuity sab mila kar. Lekin yeh aapki in-hand salary nahi hai.

Salary Structure Ke Components

1. Basic Salary

Yeh aapki salary ka sabse important part hai. Aam taur par CTC ka 40-50% hota hai. Isi par PF, HRA, aur gratuity calculate hote hain.

2. HRA (House Rent Allowance)

Basic ka 40-50% aam taur par HRA hota hai. Agar aap rent par rehte ho, to iska tax exemption milta hai.

3. Special Allowance

Yeh fully taxable hota hai. CTC ka baaki hissa yahan aata hai.

4. LTA (Leave Travel Allowance)

Travel expenses ke liye — tax exempt (conditions ke saath).

5. Other Allowances

  • Telephone/Internet: ₹1,000-2,000/month
  • Food Allowance: ₹1,000-2,000/month
  • Fuel Reimbursement: Conditions ke saath

6. Employer PF Contribution

Company aapke PF mein 12% of basic daalti hai. Yeh CTC ka part hai lekin aapko monthly nahi milta.

7. Gratuity

4.81% of basic — 5 saal complete karne par milta hai. Yeh bhi CTC ka part hai.

Deductions Kya Hote Hain?

1. Employee PF

Aapki basic ka 12% PF mein jaata hai. Yeh aapki salary se kata jaata hai.

2. Professional Tax

State government ka tax — ₹200/month (₹2,400/year). Har state mein alag.

3. Income Tax (TDS)

Aapki annual income ke hisaab se tax kata jaata hai. New regime mein ₹7 lakh tak zero tax.

4. Other Deductions

  • Health insurance premium
  • Loan EMI
  • NPS contribution
  • Salary advance

Salary Slip Kaise Padhein?

Aapki salary slip mein yeh sections hote hain:

  • Earnings: Basic, HRA, Special Allowance, etc.
  • Deductions: PF, PT, TDS, etc.
  • Net Pay: Earnings - Deductions = In-Hand

Kya Karein?

  • CTC ko in-hand na samjhein — 20-25% kam hoga
  • Basic zyada negotiate karein — PF, HRA isi par depend karte hain
  • Tax planning karein — 80C, 80D use karein
  • Salary slip save karein — loan, visa ke liye chahiye hoti hai

Conclusion

Salary structure samajhna zaroori hai — taaki aap apni salary ka sahi planning kar sakein. CTC aur in-hand mein 20-25% ka farak hota hai. Isliye job switch karte waqt CTC nahi, in-hand dekho.
