SIP vs Lumpsum: Kya Chunein?

Mutual fund investment ka sahi tarika — pura comparison

Last updated: January 2026 | Reading time: 5 minutes

Jab bhi mutual fund mein invest karne ki baat aati hai, ek sawaal zaroor aata hai — SIP karein ya Lumpsum? Dono ke apne fayde hain, aur sahi choice aapki situation par depend karti hai.

Is article mein hum dono ko detail mein samjhenge — examples ke saath.

SIP Kya Hai?

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) matlab har mahine ek fix amount invest karna. Jaise ₹5,000 per month. Yeh rupee cost averaging ka fayda deta hai — jab market neeche hota hai to zyada units milte hain, jab upar hota hai to kam.

Fayde:

  • Discipline banta hai — har mahine automatic invest
  • Market timing ki zaroorat nahi
  • Small amount se shuru kar sakte ho (₹500 se)
  • Volatility ka fayda milta hai

Nuksan:

  • Market tezi se badhe to kam units milte hain
  • Long-term commitment chahiye

Lumpsum Kya Hai?

Lumpsum matlab ek saath pura paisa invest karna. Jaise ₹5 lakh ek hi baar. Yeh compounding ka fayda deta hai — kyunki pura paisa shuru se hi grow karta hai.

Fayde:

  • Compounding ka fayda — pura paisa grow karta hai
  • Market tezi se badhe to zyada returns
  • Ek baar ka kaam, phir bhool jao

Nuksan:

  • Market timing galat ho to nuksan
  • Bada amount chahiye
  • Emotional decision ka risk

Kya Karein? — Smart Strategy

Best Approach: Dono ka combination!

  • Salaried ho? SIP karo — har mahine salary se
  • Bonus mila? Lumpsum invest karo — lekin 3-4 tranches mein (STP)
  • Market high hai? SIP karo
  • Market crash hua? Lumpsum invest karo

STP — Best of Both

STP (Systematic Transfer Plan) ek smart tarika hai — Lumpsum amount ko liquid fund mein daalo, phir har mahine thoda-thoda equity fund mein transfer karo. Isse market timing ka risk kam ho jata hai.

Conclusion

SIP aur Lumpsum — dono ke apne fayde hain. Aapki situation, cash flow, aur risk appetite ke hisaab se choose karo. Agar confusion ho to STP best hai.
