Old vs New Tax Regime: Kaunsa Better Hai?

FY 2025-26 ke liye pura comparison — examples ke saath

Last updated: January 2026 | Reading time: 6 minutes

Har saal ki tarah is saal bhi taxpayers ke saamne ek badi confusion hai — Old Tax Regime chunein ya New Tax Regime? Budget 2025 mein New Regime mein kuch badlav kiye gaye hain, jisse yeh choice aur bhi important ho gayi hai.

Is article mein hum aasan bhasha mein samjhenge ki dono regimes kya hain, kaunse slabs hain, aur aapke liye kaunsa better hai.

Old Tax Regime Kya Hai?

Old Tax Regime woh hai jo India mein decades se chala aa raha hai. Isme aap kai deductions claim kar sakte hain, jaise:

  • Section 80C — ₹1,50,000 tak (PF, LIC, ELSS, PPF, etc.)
  • Section 80D — ₹25,000 tak (health insurance)
  • Section 24(b) — ₹2,00,000 tak (home loan interest)
  • Section 80CCD(1B) — ₹50,000 tak (NPS)
  • HRA exemption
  • Standard deduction — ₹50,000

New Tax Regime Kya Hai?

New Tax Regime Budget 2020 mein launch hua tha. Isme kam tax rates hain, lekin deductions nahi milte (kuch exceptions ke saath).

Standard deduction: ₹75,000 (New Regime mein bhi)
Rebate 87A: ₹7,00,000 tak income par zero tax

Simple Rule: Kaunsa Chunein?

New Regime chunein agar:

  • Aapki income ₹7 lakh se kam hai (zero tax)
  • Aap deductions nahi karte (ya kam karte hain)
  • Aap simple tax filing chahte hain

Old Regime chunein agar:

  • Aapke paas ₹3 lakh+ deductions hain (80C + 80D + home loan)
  • Aap HRA claim karte hain
  • Aapka home loan chal raha hai
